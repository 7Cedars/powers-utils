## Q3 — Mandate Selection

The table corroborates, from a third independent jurisdiction, the same monotonic relationship
already established from Switzerland and the UAE (`switzerland_legal_forms_overview_2023.md` Q3):
liability exposure and formal governance structure scale together. Sole proprietorships and
general partnerships carry unlimited personal liability with minimal structural requirements; the
limited partnership splits liability by role (general vs. limited partners, matching
`switzerland_limited_partnership_2023.md` Q1's risk/control coupling); corporations (C-corp,
S-corp) and LLCs cap owner liability but require a formal governance layer (board of directors for
corporations, an operating agreement for LLCs) and carry the highest filing fees. This is a third,
independent data point for the general principle that liability protection and governance
formality are bundled, not independently selectable — worth citing alongside the Swiss and UAE
sources as convergent, cross-jurisdictional evidence rather than as new content on its own.
